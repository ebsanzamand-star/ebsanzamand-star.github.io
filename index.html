About

He shoots it, cuts it and runs the set — whatever the project needs.

Dipto is a Dhaka-based filmmaker with hands-on experience across official commissions, branded campaigns and independent shorts. He's comfortable in every stage of production — from pre-production planning to colour grade — and at home whether he's AD-ing on set, operating camera, or finishing a cut in DaVinci Resolve.
