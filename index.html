About

I'm Ebsan Zaman Dipto — a filmmaker, cinematographer and editor based in Dhaka. I'm currently completing my BSS in Media Studies & Journalism, majoring in Digital Film & Television Production, at the University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh. I'm drawn to visual storytelling in every form, and I'm just as happy on set as I am finishing a cut in the edit bay.

My goal is to build a career as an editor — shaping stories in the edit bay is where I feel most at home — while continuing to write, shoot and direct my own films alongside that work.
