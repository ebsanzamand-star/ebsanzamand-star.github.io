A Silent Crowd

MSJ2231 Visual Communication · Summer 2024
Project Overview

An installation using origami figures — a black rabbit hanging a green turtle from a dry tree while other turtles watch helplessly — to depict how powerful people suppress the ideas and efforts of others.
