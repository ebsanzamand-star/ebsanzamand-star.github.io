Mask Making · Visual Art

MSJ2201 Mass Communication
Submitted: Summer 2023

GEF1202 Advance English Writing Skills

10
Script Writing

Collector by the Lake

MSJ3132 Writing for Film and Television
Submitted: Fall 2024

A short screenplay about a mentally unstable man who collects discarded items — including human bones — from a lakeside, eventually building a sculpture from them. A painter, his only regular visitor, later discovers the man has disappeared, possibly drowned, and experiences a strange, dreamlike encounter. The story blurs the line between obsession, guilt, and identity.

13
Short Documentary

আহত পাখি

MSJ4131 Documentary Production
Submitted: Summer 2025

A documentary exploring the aftermath of the July 2024 protests in Bangladesh through the personal story of Shumon, a rickshaw puller from Badda, Dhaka, and his son Emon, who was shot in the leg during the movement. The film tracks the family's ongoing struggle with medical costs and daily life even after receiving some government compensation.

14
Short Film · Fiction

নূপুর

MSJ4132 Fictional Narrative Production
Submitted: Spring 2025

A dream-sequence narrative following Rafi, a man haunted by the memory of losing his partner, symbolized through a recurring anklet. Shot entirely in a single carefully composed master frame resembling a cage — representing how social conditioning can trap a person in a repeating emotional loop. The film weaves in a subplot about class oppression and a labor protest.
